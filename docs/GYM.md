# Gym: your plan, workouts and levels

Turn Gym on in **Settings → Modules / Features**. The first time you open it, Gym asks a few short questions and builds a weekly plan for you. You can skip and come back later with **✨ Build my plan**.

## The questions

One question per screen, with a progress bar and Back/Continue. Single-choice questions move on as soon as you tap an answer.

1. **About you:** age (18+), height, weight (kg or lb) and a calorie formula (female, male, or "prefer not to say", which uses the average of both). These are used only to estimate nutrition.
2. **Daily activity:** mostly sitting, lightly active, moderately active or very active.
3. **Main goal:** build muscle, get stronger, lose fat, or get fitter and healthier.
4. **Experience:** new (under 3 months), some (3–12 months) or experienced (1+ year). This sets how hard the exercises are.
5. **Where:** at a gym or at home.
6. **Kind of workouts:**
   - at a gym: machines, free weights, bodyweight or a mix;
   - at home: bodyweight only, or dumbbells.
7. **What you enjoy:** strength training, cardio, core and abs, stretching and mobility. Optionally pick up to 2 focus areas, such as arms or legs and glutes.
8. **When:** training days (1–6; at least one rest day), session length (20–90 min) and usual time of day.

The last screen reviews every answer (each has **Edit**). It also asks whether to include calorie and nutrient targets. Change your answers any time with **✎ Change my answers**: your XP, level and history are kept.

## Your plan

- **My Plan** (the Gym overview) shows:
  - today's challenge, with every exercise listed, a warm-up and a cool-down;
  - your level, XP and discipline;
  - this week (done ✓, missed ✕, made up ↻, planned •, rest ☾);
  - today's nutrition and recent XP.
- **Workouts** lists each training day in full. You can:
  - swap any exercise (⇄) for another that suits your equipment;
  - add a plan day to your timetable (it repeats weekly at your chosen time);
  - start the workout.

  Saved workouts, your own routines, the Gym A / B examples, history and the exercise library are on the same page.
- **Splits:**
  - 1–3 days: full-body workouts. With some experience, 3 days becomes push / pull / legs.
  - 4 days: upper / lower.
  - 5 days: upper, lower, push, pull, legs.
  - 6 days: push / pull / legs twice.
  - If you didn't choose strength training, sessions are cardio, core and/or stretching.
- **Sets and reps follow your goal:**
  - strength: 5–8 reps with long rests;
  - muscle: 8–12 reps;
  - fat loss and fitness: 10–15 reps with short rests.

  Beginners only get beginner-level exercises, and home plans never use gym equipment.
- **Exercise pictures:** every exercise has one. Tap an exercise for an animated demonstration (start → finish), step-by-step tips, muscles worked and equipment.

## During a workout

All exercises are listed at once, not one at a time:
- Each set has its own row: type the weight and reps, then tick ✓. The set is saved straight away.
- After each set a rest timer starts with the planned rest time; use **+30 s** or **Skip rest**.
- You can add a set or an exercise, change the date, or finish.
- Finishing shows the XP you earned and why.

## Levels, XP and discipline

You earn XP for every workout. How much depends on your **discipline**: how reliably you train on the days *you* planned. It is not about training every day.

| What happens | Discipline |
|---|---|
| Train on a planned day (at least half of its sets) | +10 |
| Miss a planned day | −25 |
| Can't make it, so train the next day (if that day is a rest day) | no change |
| Do less than half of a planned day's sets | no change |
| Extra workout on a rest day | no change (earns up to 50 XP) |
| Plan paused (exams, illness, travel) | no change; paused days never count as missed |

- **XP per workout:** up to 100 XP × your multiplier. The multiplier runs from **×0.5** (discipline 0) to **×1.5** (discipline 100); you start at 50 (×1.0).
- **Partly done:** a workout with only some of the sets earns XP for the part you did.
- **One planned workout per day:** a second workout on the same planned day adds no extra XP.

**Example (Mon / Wed / Fri plan):**
- Train all three: Mon +100, Wed +110, Fri +120 = **330 XP**.
- Skip Wednesday: Mon +100, Wed ✕, Fri +85 = **185 XP**.

The same Friday workout earns less because Wednesday was missed.

- **Levels:** Level 1 → 2 needs 250 XP, and each level needs 150 XP more than the one before. Titles go Rookie, Starter, Regular, Committed, Athlete, Strong, Elite, Champion, Legend.
- **Always matches your history:** XP is recalculated from your logged workouts every time, so it always matches your history. Changing your plan only affects days from then on.

## Nutrition

**Nutrition** shows what your body needs each day:
- calories, protein, carbs, fat, water and fibre;
- what each one is for, food ideas, and a per-meal split.

How the numbers are estimated:
- **Calories:** Mifflin–St Jeor with your activity level. Building muscle adds 10%, losing fat removes 10%.
- **Protein:** 1.4–1.8 g per kg.
- **Fat and carbs:** fat is 30% of calories; carbs make up the rest.
- **Water:** about 35 ml per kg.
- **Fibre:** 14 g per 1000 kcal.

These are general adult estimates, not medical advice. They are not for pregnancy, breastfeeding or medical diets. Log meals and adjust the numbers with the calculator on the same page.

## Privacy and safety

Everything is stored in your own account's local database. The plan and tips are general guidance, not personal coaching. Move with control, and stop if you feel pain or unusual symptoms.
